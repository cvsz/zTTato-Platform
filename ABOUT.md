# zTTato Creator

zTTato is a creator-controlled web app for connecting TikTok accounts, preparing video content, uploading drafts and choosing an explicit Direct Post action. Website: https://zttato.zeaz.dev. GitHub owner: cvsz. Category: Photo & Video.

The Legal & Review Preview is an illustrative browser mockup, not an integration or substitute for a real TikTok Sandbox recording. TikTok and zTTato are independent services.
